My Journey

Growing up with my dad working in the computer science industry, technology was a constant in my life. Between gaming, computers, and tinkering, developing an interest in coding felt like second nature, which led me to transfer into Computer Science during my freshman year at Purdue.

At the same time, an introductory psychology class I took during my senior year of high school had sparked an enduring fascination with cognitive behavior and how human minds process information. When I discovered I could combine both passions through a double major, everything clicked. In the spring of my sophomore year, I transferred from CS into Artificial Intelligence (B.S.), formally uniting computational rigor with cognitive psychology.

Since then, I've been exploring how these fields intersect, especially within machine learning. Double majoring allows me to view ML not just as abstract math equations, but as agents operating on incentives, feedback loops, and learning dynamics.

Career Aspirations

My primary goal is to work in Machine Learning and Applied AI Engineering. I want to be directly involved in designing, training, and deploying models, especially in reinforcement learning, deep learning, and autonomous systems.

Whether it's building intelligent simulation agents, optimizing decision-making algorithms, or developing AI tools that solve real-world engineering challenges, I'm eager to join a team where I can contribute to core model development and scale practical AI solutions.

Global Presence

Travel Geography

To me, traveling isn't just about crossing destinations off a checklist; it's about stepping completely outside my comfort zone and immersing myself in different cultures, lifestyles, and ways of thinking. Whether it's navigating the hyper-connected, synchronized transit systems of Japan, exploring ancient history in Rome, venturing into the red heart of Australia at Uluru, or experiencing the vibrant community warmth in Costa Rica, Mexico, and Puerto Rico, every trip reshapes how I view the world. Coming from a background in psychology and artificial intelligence, experiencing diverse environments firsthand gives me an irreplaceable appreciation for how human beings communicate, adapt, and build community across different traditions. Traveling keeps me curious, grounded, and adaptable, constantly reminding me that no matter where you go, there is always something new to learn from the people around you.
